# "One Family" — PetPlan Compare Summer Commercial

**Format:** :60 broadcast/social cutdown (with :30 and :15 trim points marked)
**Style:** Japanese animation (Studio Ghibli / Makoto Shinkai-inspired) — soft watercolor backgrounds, warm hand-painted light, gentle character animation, no violence or peril, high emotional warmth
**Season:** Summer — golden hour, cicadas, breeze, sunflower fields, seaside town
**Palette:** Warm cream, soft gold (#c9a96e — brand gold), sky blue, blush pink, sunset orange. Matches the PetPlan Compare brand mark (gold paw print) and wordmark (italic "PetPlan" + spaced "COMPARE").

> Note on the brief: no script can *guarantee* "millions of dollars" — that depends on media spend, placement, and distribution, not the script alone. What I can do is write the strongest, most emotionally resonant :60 I can, built specifically around your real site (the gold paw logo, the animal types you support, and your actual 3-step quote flow) so it's ready to hand to an animation studio or AI video pipeline.

## COLD OPEN — "The Window" (0:00–0:06)

*Golden late-afternoon light through a lace curtain. A shiba-inu-like dog naps in a sunbeam on a wooden windowsill, ear twitching. Dust motes drift like fireflies. Distant cicada hum, soft wind chimes.*

**VO (warm, gentle, almost a lullaby):**
"Every pet has a person... who'd do anything for them."

*The dog wakes, stretches, hops down — camera follows at floor level as it trots toward a door.*

---

## SCENE 1 — "The Street" (0:06–0:16)

*Wide painterly shot: a sun-drenched summer street, laundry lines swaying, a popsicle cart. Three friends of different backgrounds — a Black woman with locs, a South Asian man with a cane, an East Asian teenager on a bike — each walking with their pets: a cat riding in a bike basket, a rabbit in a tote bag, a parrot on a shoulder.*

*They converge at a corner, laughing, comparing something on their phones. Screens glow with the soft gold **PetPlan Compare paw mark**.*

**VO:**
"Different pets. Different families. Same worry — *is my best friend covered?*"

*Cut: close-up on the parrot nuzzling the teenager's cheek. Then the rabbit's nose twitching against the woman's palm.*

## SCENE 2 — "The Reveal" (0:16–0:26)

*Match-cut: all three phone screens tilt into frame together, forming a single glowing triptych. On each, the actual site loads — hero line **"Compare top pet insurance plans for dogs, cats, birds, reptiles, and exotic animals — in seconds."***

*The gold paw-print logo animates in: big paw, then small paw, tracing itself like a brushstroke — then the wordmark settles: **PetPlan** *(italic script)* **COMPARE** *(spaced caps)*.*

**VO (warmer, more confident now):**
"PetPlan Compare. One free look — every plan, side by side."

*On-screen text overlay (clean, matches site type): "100% Free to Compare"*

---

## SCENE 3 — "The Walkthrough" (0:26–0:40)

*Soft UI-style animation, illustrated in the same painterly hand — not a sterile screen recording, but drawn as if the app lives inside the film's world. Three floating lanterns, each showing one step (mirrors the site's real 3-step "How It Works" flow):*

1. *Lantern 1 — an icon of a paw tapping "Dog / Cat / Bird / Rabbit / Reptile / Exotic" → the dog from the cold open wags its tail as its silhouette is chosen.*
2. *Lantern 2 — a few gentle taps (age, breed, zip) — cherry blossoms/petals fall with each tap like turning pages.*
3. *Lantern 3 — plans bloom into view like flowers opening, gold price tags glowing softly, a big satisfying **"Get My Free Quote"** button blooms last, brightest.*

**VO:**
"No agents. No pressure. Just answers — in under a minute."

*Sound design: a soft chime on each tap, building into a warm musical swell.*

A fourth friend — an older Latino man with a tortoise in a basket — joins, waving. Fireworks begin, tiny and distant, over the rooftops (summer festival).*

*Each pet, in turn, looks directly-ish toward camera with a soft blink — dog, cat, parrot, rabbit, tortoise — like a family portrait.*

## CUTDOWN GUIDE

- **:30 version:** Open on Scene 1 (trimmed to 4s) → Scene 2 (full) → Scene 3 (compressed to two lanterns, ~8s) → Closing logo moment (full).
- **:15 version:** Scene 2 reveal → Closing logo moment only. Pure brand + CTA punch.

## PRODUCTION NOTES

- **Voice casting:** one warm, gender-neutral-leaning VO artist across all cuts for consistency; consider a soft acoustic guitar + music-box melody bed (think summer-festival lo-fi, not orchestral bombast).
- **Animal roster** matches your actual supported types (dog, cat, bird, rabbit, reptile, exotic) so the visuals never overpromise past what the product covers.
- **UI moments are illustrated, not screen-captured** — keeps the anime tone intact while still teaching the real flow (animal → details → instant plans) and the real CTA copy ("Compare Now" / "Compare Plans →") from your site.
- **Diversity casting note:** the four friends are written as distinct individuals (mobility aid, different ages, different ethnicities) participating as equals in one shared scene, not a checklist cutaway — keeps the "coming together as friends" feel earned rather than tokenized.
- **Where this plugs into growth:** the script alone won't move revenue — pair it with: a landing page A/B test against this creative, retargeting cutdowns (:15/:6) for social, and tracking quote-starts from ad traffic so you can prove (or disprove) lift before scaling spend.
